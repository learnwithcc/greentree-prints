What the trade side makes

Small runs that a big shop won't bother quoting.

Three kinds of work, each priced per job. Bring a logo, a wheel model or a rough count; I'll tell you what's possible before you commit to anything.

Pour concrete for a living?

A name stamp with your company name or logo, pressed into every sidewalk panel you finish. Ask for a swappable year insert and next January's update is a small part, not a whole new stamp.

Spin on a wheel with pricey bobbins?

Bobbins in sets of three, made for your wheel's make and model. Some wheels I've fit-checked and some I haven't; I'll tell you which before you pay.

Want your logo on a short run?

Equipment ID tags, keychains, desk signs, cable clips, the jig your crew keeps losing. Ten pieces or a hundred, quoted with a proof of your logo first.

Why a separate address

A drawer knob needs a link. A stamp needs a logo and a proof.

Neighbourhood requests on this site are built around a link, a photo and a porch pickup. Trade jobs need more: a logo file, quantities, a deadline, sometimes shipping. The Pro Desk at greentreeprints.pro has a quote form built for exactly that, and an address short enough for a business card or a truck door.
