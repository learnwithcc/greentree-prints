The honest fine print

        
    
          
  • Nothing prints without a yes. You approve the price and the proof first. If the proof isn't right, we fix the proof, not the stamp.
    • 
          
  • Printed isn't bronze. I can't yet show you a printed stamp outlasting bronze over a season of pours. If that matters, order one and try it on a test slab first.
    • 
          
  • Small batches only. This is one shop with one queue. If your run is bigger than I can do well, I'll say so in the quote, not after.
    • 
          
  • Canada only, for now. I ship within Canada and offer pickup in Burnaby. No US orders until I've sorted out the customs side.
    • 
          
  • Rush costs more. Rush jobs jump the queue and cost a little more; that priority is what you're paying for.
    • 
          
  • Prices are in Canadian dollars. Every quote is a firm number in CAD, so there's no exchange-rate guessing.
