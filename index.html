The honest fine print

        
    
          
  • Jobs start at $10. Small parts are cheap; you're mostly paying for setup and care, not plastic.
    • 
          
  • Materials matter. Everyday items get PLA. Anything for the car, garage, or outdoors gets PETG so it doesn't warp in the heat.
    • 
          
  • Safety parts are a no. No car seat components, crib hardware, or anything a person's safety hangs on. I'd rather lose the job.
    • 
          
  • Some things need a real designer. If your idea needs custom modelling beyond my depth, I'll say so and point you somewhere good.
    • 
          
  • Colour library is growing. Common colours are in stock; special requests may add a few days.
    • 
          
  • This is a home shop. One printer, real quality checks, honest timelines. If something's going to take longer, you'll hear it from me first.
